Knowledge Need
Type: Structure
What It Does
Knowledge Need helps the owner clarify why inquiry is being undertaken. It begins
with the available input—business situation—and develops question frame and
research plan.
Use It When
Use Knowledge Need when business situation is available but the business still
needs question frame and research plan. It is most useful when that output will
change what you investigate, compare, decide or do next.
Do Not Use It When
Do not use it merely because the name is familiar. If the required starting material
is unavailable or the output will not change the present work, choose another tool
or stop.

How to Use It
1.
Name the work or decision the structure will preserve.
2.
Populate each field from available evidence and owner judgment.
3.
Mark assumptions, unknowns, disagreement and missing items explicitly.
4.
Review the record for completeness and approve its next use.
What It Produces
The completed work produces question frame and research plan. The result should
be clear enough that another person can see what was considered, what remains
uncertain and what the output is ready to support.
Connects To
Begins with: Business situation. This is the material the tool receives from earlier
work or directly from the business situation.
Produces: Question Frame; research plan. This becomes available to the next
method, owner review or responsible action.
Usually leads to: Work that can use question frame and research plan to build a
stated purpose and knowledge need. If the output does not support the intended
next step, return to the starting material, correct the boundary or select another
tool.
Connection to Volume One: Part Two, Chapter 1.
Business Example
A repair company notices several kinds of customer complaints and needs to decide
what it is actually trying to learn before collecting more information. The owner
uses Knowledge Need to turn the available input into question frame and research
plan. The result is then reviewed or passed into the next connected tool instead of
being treated as a conclusion by itself.
