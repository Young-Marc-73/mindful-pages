Important Assumptions
An assumption is something being treated as true without enough evidence to call it a 
fact. Assumptions are unavoidable. Hidden assumptions are dangerous because 
decisions can depend on them without anyone recognizing the dependence.
Questions You Can Use to Dig Deeper
•  What must be true for the business to work as currently understood?
•  Which belief would cause the most trouble if it proved false?
•  What comes from evidence, and what comes from hope or habit?
•  Which assumption should become a research question?
The Working Picture
The Working Picture brings the section together. It is your dated interpretation of the 
business based on the best information currently available. It should preserve 
uncertainty instead of hiding it and remain open to revision when new knowledge 
appears.
Questions You Can Use to Dig Deeper
•  What appears to be true now?
•  What evidence supports that view?
•  Where do the pieces agree or conflict?
•  What remains uncertain?
•  Which part of this signal is still the least understood?
•  What question should be carried into the Research Journal?
•  What may be clear enough to carry into the Planner?
